===== Rule.natural_readings_that_differ.r1_scope | Rule: For a batch for which every transfer of the batch is not valid on the ledger, no transfer is applied
applies to  a batch: every ledger, every batch (a list of transfers, in order)
            every such call, accepted or rejected [default D2]
assuming    every transfer of the batch can NOT be carried out exactly on the ledger (an account does not exist, or both
            are the same, or the sender does not cover the amount, or the receiver's balance would pass 2^64 - 1)
then        the call gives an answer [default D1]
            the number of transfers the call reports as applied is 0
defaults    D1 always answers: the call must return an answer for every input the reading applies to (no panic, no
            abort, no endless loop)
            D2 rejected calls are covered: the reading applies to every call, whether the operation accepts or rejects
            it
            D4 for all values: every outline <parameter>, every state and every input is universally quantified at the
            front; the Examples rows are instances
            D5 collection words bind inside their own clause: 'every', 'the first', 'no' range over the named collection
            within the one Given, Then or Rule clause they occur in
            D8 conditions are read before the call: what 'assuming', 'for which', 'whenever', 'only if' and 'exactly
            when' say about the state is judged on the state before the call (of a sequence: before its first call), not
            on any state the call passes through or leaves
            D9 negation binds to the one condition it is written on: 'every X is not P' means that no X is P, never 'not
            every X is P'
===== Rule.natural_readings_that_differ.r2_whenever | Rule: A withdrawal is accepted whenever the amount is not more than the wallet's balance
applies to  a withdrawal: every wallet, every amount (a u64: 0 to 2^64 - 1)
            every such call, accepted or rejected [default D2]
assuming    nothing: every state and every input
then        the call gives an answer [default D1]
            if the amount is at most the balance of the wallet, then the answer is an acceptance (see `accepted`) (and
            the call gives an answer whether or not the amount is at most the balance of the wallet)
accepted    an acceptance is the answer Ok; an Err answer is a rejection
defaults    D1 always answers: the call must return an answer for every input the reading applies to (no panic, no
            abort, no endless loop)

===== Rule.natural_readings_that_differ.r3_timing | Rule: A batch applied as a whole is accepted exactly when every transfer of the batch is valid on the ledger
applies to  a batch applied as a whole: every ledger, every batch (a list of transfers, in order)
            every such call, accepted or rejected [default D2]
assuming    nothing: every state and every input
then        the call gives an answer [default D1]
            the answer is an acceptance (see `accepted`) if and only if every transfer of the batch can be carried out
            exactly on the ledger (both accounts exist and differ, the sender covers the amount, the receiver's balance
            stays within 2^64 - 1)
accepted    an acceptance is the answer yes (true); the answer no is a rejection
defaults    D1 always answers: the call must return an answer for every input the reading applies to (no panic, no

===== Rule.natural_readings_that_differ.r4_rejected_batch | Rule: A rejected batch never changes the total of all balances of the ledger
applies to  a batch: every ledger, every batch (a list of transfers, in order)
            ONLY rejected calls [asked for]
assuming    nothing: every state and every input
then        the sum of the balances of every account of the ledger after the call equals the sum of the balances of
            every account of the ledger before it
accepted    every answer is an acceptance: applying a batch transfer by transfer reports a count and never rejects, so a
            clause about rejected calls covers no call
defaults    D3 before / after one call: 'unchanged', 'increased by' and the like compare the state before one call with
            the state after it, not states in between
            D4 for all values: every outline <parameter>, every state and every input is universally quantified at the
            front; the Examples rows are instances
not default ONLY rejected calls are covered (asked for by 'rejected' / 'if it is rejected'); calls that are accepted, or
            that give no answer, are outside this clause
