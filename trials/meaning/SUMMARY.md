## Method

- A reader judges each item faithful or not-faithful. An unfaithful item is *caught* when the reader says not-faithful; a *false alarm* is a faithful item the reader says is not-faithful. The reason a reader gives is recorded but not marked: a catch for the wrong reason still counts as a catch.
- Intervals are 95% Wilson score intervals. With 18 unfaithful items, one reader who catches all of them has an interval of [82.4%, 100.0%], so a single reader cannot be placed above or below a 90% target.
- Proxy and human readers are scored by the same code and never pooled together.
- Readers who have answered only some items are scored on the items they answered.
- Re-run `python3 score.py` after adding a file to `responses/`.
