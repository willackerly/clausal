How it behaves:

- **No model.** Tokenise, anchor operations by noun, fill typed holes of the book's forms through the existing loader (`Parser.forms_for`, `Parser.lexical`, `op_roles`), rank by a stated rule, compile.
- **Guarantee.** `compose.checked` recompiles every candidate just before output and raises if one is refused or comes back as another Lean term. The reading shown is the compiler's echo.
- **Interactive mode.** The top three are compared pairwise through `separate.separate`; the separating input and a question are printed. Nothing is chosen without `--choose K` or an answer at the prompt.
- **`--json`.** Ranked candidates, `chosen: 0`, "rank (no human choice)".
- **`--write FILE.feature`.** Needs a chosen candidate, compiles the file afterwards and puts it back on failure.
- **Model hook.** `compose.model_hook` is the marked place; it returns nothing and `compose` asserts that.
