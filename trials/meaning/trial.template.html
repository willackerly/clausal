Meaning trial

You will read a short brief and then judge items, one at a time. Each item shows two texts; you say whether the second says exactly the same thing as the first, how sure you are, and why.

You will not be told whether your answers are right. Please do not look anything up or discuss the items with other readers.

You can stop at any time. Your progress is kept in this browser, and you can also save a partial answers file and load it later. Nothing is sent anywhere: at the end you download a file and send it to the person running the trial.
