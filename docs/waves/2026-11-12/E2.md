## Defect 2: the property-test size was understated for two-call rules

**Fix.** The runner (`clausal/evidence/src/main.rs`) now records the size itself, per reading:

- `limit` is the longest ledger and the longest batch its generators can produce. It is `--size` for a free variable, the literal's length for one a Given fixes, and the first call's limit plus `TWEAK_ACCOUNTS` (2) or `TWEAK_TRANSFERS` (1) for a second call. `tweak` now uses those same constants.
- `longest` is the longest collection among the cases in scope that passed.

`sizes.assess` reports both, per kind of collection, instead of `evidence.SIZE`. A row with no `limit` reads "limit not recorded" and gives no number. `sizes.overdrawn` fails the run if any reading drew more than its stated limit. Control `c42` restores the understatement in the runner and expects that error.

The other two "collections up to 6 items" texts were also touched. The generated-evidence table now adds "(up to 8 accounts in the second call of a two-call rule)". The matching addition in the per-rule property-test row (`rules.py`) does not show up: `grep` still finds "collections up to 6 items;" in rule blocks at `out/report.md` lines 316 and 847, and I did not check why.
