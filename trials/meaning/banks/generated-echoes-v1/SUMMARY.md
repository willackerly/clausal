### Pooled over proxy readers

Every reader's judgement of every item counts once. The Wilson interval treats those judgements as independent, which they are not (the same items repeat across readers), so it is too narrow. The 'items resampled' interval is a bootstrap that resamples items instead (4000 draws, seed 20261110); it is the better guide to what a different item bank might give, and it collapses to a single point when every item in the row has the same result. The 'items and readers resampled' interval resamples both at once, so it also carries the spread between readers; with only a few readers that side of it is coarse (four readers give 35 distinct samples of readers), and it says nothing about readers of a different kind from those who took the trial.

## Method

- A reader judges each item faithful or not-faithful. An unfaithful item is *caught* when the reader says not-faithful; a *false alarm* is a faithful item the reader says is not-faithful. The reason a reader gives is recorded but not marked: a catch for the wrong reason still counts as a catch.
- Intervals are 95% Wilson score intervals. With 15 unfaithful items, one reader who catches all of them has an interval of [79.6%, 100.0%], so a single reader cannot be placed above or below a 90% target.
- Proxy and human readers are scored by the same code and never pooled together.
- Readers who have answered only some items are scored on the items they answered.
- Re-run `python3 score.py` after adding a file to `responses/`.
