## How it works

- **Command:** `python3 -m clausal separate "<A>" ["<B>"] [--json] [--no-rust] [--no-prove]`.
- **Compile:** each sentence goes through `Compiler.rule` once per system, using its existing `domain_hint`.
- **Search:** the existing reading search (`newrules.script`, `rsearch.search_file`) runs on the Lean model, and the two `RBITS` strings are compared point by point.
- **Replay:** the reported points are replayed on the compiled Rust. The report gives the answer, whether it was accepted, the state before and after, and whether the Rust bits equal the Lean bits.
- **Verdicts:** `SEPARATED`, `SAME-TERM`, `PROVED-EQUAL`, `NONE-FOUND` (always with the bounds printed), `DIFFERENT-OPERATIONS`, `NOT-SEARCHED`.
- **Cross-system:** a `cross_system` note appears whenever a sentence uses a noun that names operations in two systems. It says where the sentence compiles and gives the other system's refusal.
